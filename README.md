# Paola Aguirre Ruelas

**Computational Biology · Biomedical Machine Learning · Reproducible Research**

I'm a biomedical researcher with a strong interest in understanding human biological variability and how computational methods can help us extract meaningful information from complex biomedical data.

I'm particularly interested in sleep research, personalized health, and the potential of machine learning to uncover biological patterns that are difficult to capture through traditional approaches.

My current research explores the reliability, reproducibility, and generalizability of biomedical predictions, with a focus on understanding not only when models work, but also when and why they fail.

## Research principles

**Questions before models.** Define the scientific estimand and relevant data-generating process before optimizing performance.

**Generalization before claims.** Examine subject- and donor-level variation, evaluation design, and limitations of transfer to new populations.

**Evidence before conclusions.** Share methods, code, provenance, verification status, and the limits of what results establish.

## Current direction

I am developing stronger methodological foundations in machine learning for biomedical research, especially **longitudinal inference, robust representation learning, and the reliability of individual-level predictions**. A central motivation is understanding what it takes for a model that works on a dataset to provide dependable information about a new person.
