*Sleep research · Individual variability · Reproducible science*

[LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/paola-aguirre-ruelas/) · [Selected publication](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cub.2019.10.010)

I have a BSc in Genetics and Biotechnology and an MSc in Biomedicine, with experience in bioinformatics research. My work explores how biomedical data can support reliable scientific conclusions—and where aggregate model performance fails to capture uncertainty or variability at the individual level.

I am particularly interested in **sleep research, longitudinal physiological data, subject-level generalization, biomarker discovery, and personalized prediction**. I am building a portfolio of independent, reproducible computational studies as I pursue PhD research opportunities.

## Research principles

**Questions before models.** Define the scientific estimand and relevant data-generating process before optimizing performance.

**Generalization before claims.** Examine subject- and donor-level variation, evaluation design, and limitations of transfer to new populations.

**Evidence before conclusions.** Share methods, code, provenance, verification status, and the limits of what results establish.

## Current direction

I am developing stronger methodological foundations in machine learning for biomedical research, especially **longitudinal inference, robust representation learning, and the reliability of individual-level predictions**. A central motivation is understanding what it takes for a model that works on a dataset to provide dependable information about a new person.
